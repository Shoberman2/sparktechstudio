## Missing facts needed to complete an integration notice

- Exact app name, publisher/controller identity, business contact details, and confirmed privacy-request contact.
- Actual LinkedIn products and OAuth scopes; whether member profiles, email, posts, Page data, analytics, comments, messages, or other information are accessed.
- Actual purposes and which actions run for SparkTech versus a separate client such as UTern.
- Where OAuth credentials/tokens, imported data, content drafts, logs, and backups are stored; who can access each.
- Hosting, storage, email, model/AI, analytics, and other service providers actually used; what each receives and relevant locations.
- Actual retention periods, token expiration/revocation, disconnect behavior, deletion method and timing, and backup handling.
- Whether data is sent to model services or used for training, whether it is shared with any other party, and the applicable arrangements. Do not infer “no sale” or “no training”.
- Actual website tracking/cookies and hosting log practices; the absence of an analytics package in the local app does not establish the absence of host-level processing.
- User rights/request handling, intended audience, applicable jurisdictions, and an effective date after review.
