# Morning Brief — 2026-04-13

## Theme: Studio Origin Story Sprint

### Platform: LinkedIn
**Why:** Origin stories perform best where founders and builders already browse. LinkedIn organic reach for "why we exist" narrative posts consistently outperforms other platforms for B2B creative studios. This is where people with product ideas spend their Monday mornings.

### Template: Single Image + Long-form Post
**Why:** Text-heavy origin posts with one strong visual anchor outperform carousels for first-time awareness. No swipe friction. The image stops the scroll, the copy does the selling.

### Headline
**"We only take the projects other studios say no to."**

### Subheadline
Most dev studios want safe, scoped work. We want the weird ones.

### Body Copy
Every studio says they build "custom software." Most of them mean they'll slot your idea into a template and charge you agency rates for it.

We started SparkTech because we kept meeting people with genuinely wild ideas and nowhere to take them. The app that shouldn't work. The product nobody's tried. The thing your last dev team said was "out of scope" before you even finished explaining it.

That's exactly the work we want. If your idea makes people raise an eyebrow, you're our kind of client.

### CTA
"Got something wild? Tell us about it." → Link to waitlist/contact

### Visual Direction
- Warm off-white background (#fafaf7) with subtle grain texture
- Bold Playfair Display headline in dark text (#1a1a1e), left-aligned
- A single gold accent line or spark mark next to the headline
- No stock photos, no team headshots, no laptop-on-desk cliches
- Clean, typographic, feels like a craft poster pinned to a workshop wall
- SparkTech logo (spark mark) small in bottom corner with gold gradient

### Posting Time
**Monday 8:30 AM ET** — Catches the Monday morning LinkedIn scroll before meetings start. Origin story content performs best early-week when people are in planning/thinking mode.

### Confidence: 7/10
Day 1 with zero audience data. The angle is strong (contrarian positioning always gets engagement on LinkedIn) but we're flying blind on audience response. This is a baseline post. The real signal comes from what happens after we publish.

### Agent Note
Contrarian positioning ("we take what others won't") is the strongest Day 1 play for a studio with no social proof yet. It reframes having no portfolio as a feature. LinkedIn's algorithm rewards text posts with one image. Don't overthink the visual. Copy carries this one.
