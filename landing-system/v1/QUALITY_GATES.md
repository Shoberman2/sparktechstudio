# Landing page quality gates

Record pass, fail, or blocked with a short evidence note. A blocked visual or legal check must not be represented as passed.

## Identity and evidence

- The page unmistakably belongs to the target company without borrowing another company's logo, assets, interface, copy, or signature motif.
- Product claims and capability descriptions map to current implementation or an approved source.
- Customer, performance, security, regulatory, ownership, and legal claims have explicit supporting evidence.
- Every synthetic interface uses clear nearby disclosure and no real personal or customer data.
- Company data, credentials, access policy, and source material remain within that company's boundary.

## Experience

- The opening gives a concrete product outcome, a concise explanation, and a working primary action.
- Product visuals represent actual behavior or a clearly identified illustration.
- The page has a coherent narrative, useful navigation, distinct sections, and a complete footer.
- Responsive layout, keyboard access, focus visibility, contrast, semantic headings, and reduced motion are checked.
- Motion and visual effects support the target brand rather than being copied from a reference.
- No unsupported claims, broken destinations, placeholder copy, generic numbered benefits, or unexplained mock data remain.

## Delivery

- Existing dirty files are preserved.
- Applicable repository lint, type, and test checks are run and results are recorded.
- No account connection, external request, message, deployment, or publishing action occurs without separate explicit authorization.
- The final review distinguishes implemented, verified, blocked, and not attempted work.
